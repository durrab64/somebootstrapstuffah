Main Content Area

Welcome to your dashboard. This area stretches dynamically to occupy 9 columns of space when viewed on desktop and widescreen devices.

Bootstrap's 12-column grid system is extremely versatile. By applying .col-12 and .col-lg-9 to the main section alongside .col-12 and .col-lg-3 to the sidebar, we create a responsive structure. Smaller screens trigger stacking behavior naturally because .col-12 instructs the browser to fill the entire horizontal space.
