Load More Blog Posts

Track a page counter and append posts from /api/posts?page=X

How it works: A variable currentPage starts at 1. When "Load More" is clicked, we fetch /api/posts?page=1, build HTML elements for each post, append them to #blog-container, and increment the counter.
